01 / SeeMo integration

HDMI in.
Parallax on screen.

Coming in Parallax Monitor 2.1: Accsoon SeeMo support for iPhone and iPad.

Connect your camera’s HDMI output to SeeMo, then connect SeeMo to your iPhone or iPad. Monitor that picture in Parallax while controlling a supported camera over Wi-Fi.

  • LUT preview, waveform and histogram
  • False color, zebras and focus peaking
  • Exposure lights, frame guides and anamorphic desqueeze
  • Switch between SeeMo and the camera’s own preview
View Parallax Monitor for iOS ↗

SeeMo support is not included in the current App Store version. It requires Parallax Monitor 2.1 or later, compatible SeeMo hardware and an iPhone or iPad. Hardware sold separately. Camera controls depend on the camera and its Wi-Fi connection.

Before you build your rig

A few useful answers.

Which integration should I choose?

SeeMo support is coming in Parallax Monitor 2.1 for monitoring on an iPhone or iPad. Choose the Accsoon edition of ZR Ctl if you want the app to run directly on a CineView M7.

Does the Accsoon purchase include the iPhone or Google Play app?

No. The Accsoon edition is a separate purchase for your monitor. Parallax Monitor for iPhone and iPad is available through the App Store, and the Android phone/tablet app is available through Google Play.

Does HDMI also control the camera?

The HDMI connection supplies the picture. Camera control uses a separate supported connection. For the SeeMo workflow, connect the camera to Parallax over Wi-Fi as well as connecting the HDMI video path.

Does Parallax record the SeeMo feed?

The SeeMo integration displays the video for monitoring. It does not record the SeeMo feed or stream it to the internet. Recording controls operate a connected, supported camera.

How many monitors can I activate?

Your Accsoon license includes three device activations and never expires. Lifetime updates are included. You can manage your activations through My purchases.

Where are my download and license key?

Open My purchases and sign in with the email used at checkout. For help with installation, activation or your camera setup, contact support.
