Labels and markings for Parallax Monitor

Last updated: July 2, 2026

This page provides product safety and compliance labels or markings information for Parallax Monitor, formerly ZR Ctl on iOS, and related ZR Ctl software products.

Product Identification

  • Product: Parallax Monitor
  • Related product name: ZR Ctl
  • Product type: Mobile software application for Nikon Z camera monitoring and control
  • Developer: Mike Aguado / MAD Creative

EU Labels and Markings

Parallax Monitor is distributed as software only. It is not a physical product and does not include physical packaging, batteries, radio equipment, electrical or electronic hardware, toys, personal protective equipment, medical devices, or other goods that would carry product safety labels or physical compliance markings.

Based on the current software-only product, no EU product safety labels or physical compliance markings are applied to Parallax Monitor itself.

Markings Not Used for This Software Product

  • CE marking: Not applied to the app itself because the app is software and not hardware placed on the market.
  • WEEE marking: Not applied to the app itself because the app is not electrical or electronic equipment.
  • Battery marking: Not applied to the app itself because the app does not include a battery product.
  • Packaging labels: Not applied to the app itself because the app is distributed digitally and has no physical packaging.

Safety Information

Parallax Monitor and ZR Ctl are camera monitoring and control applications. They do not replace the safety instructions, warnings, markings, or compliance information supplied by camera, device, cable, mount, power, or accessory manufacturers.

Use cameras, mobile devices, cables, mounts, batteries, and accessories according to their manufacturers' instructions. Secure all equipment before use and avoid operating camera equipment in unsafe situations.

Regulated Product Status

Parallax Monitor is not intended for medical diagnosis, treatment, emergency communication, safety-critical control, or use as a regulated medical device.
