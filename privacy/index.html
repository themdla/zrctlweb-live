Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for ZR Ctl

Last updated: May 10, 2026

ZR Ctl is developed by Mike Aguado.

ZR Ctl does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to the developer or to third parties during normal use.

Data the App Accesses

ZR Ctl connects directly to supported Nikon cameras over local network, Wi-Fi, USB, or related device connection features. The app may access camera status, camera settings, live view data, media metadata, and control commands only to provide camera monitoring and control features.

This data is used locally on your device and is not sent to the developer or any third-party service.

Local Storage

ZR Ctl may store app preferences, camera connection settings, pairing identifiers, imported LUTs, cached thumbnails, diagnostic logs, and similar app data locally on your device. This information remains on your device unless you choose to share it.

Deleting the app deletes locally stored app data handled by the operating system.

Support Emails

If you choose to send logs to support, ZR Ctl may prepare an email containing diagnostic information such as app version, device model, operating system version, camera name, connection state, and recent app logs. This information is only sent if you choose to send the email.

Support requests may be retained as needed to respond to the request. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the developer.

Third Parties

ZR Ctl does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking services.

Children

ZR Ctl is not directed to children but contains no illicit content.

Contact

Changes
