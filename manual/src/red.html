Chapter 16

RED cameras.

The app controls RED KOMODO, KOMODO-X and V-RAPTOR bodies over RED's RCP2 protocol and monitors their live picture with every tool. This chapter covers what is different from a Nikon.

BodiesKOMODO, KOMODO-X, V-RAPTOR and V-RAPTOR XL families. Not DSMC2 or older
LinkWi-Fi, or a USB-C network tether (the original KOMODO needs the KOMODO Link Adapter)
EvidenceBuilt against RED's protocol; field reports are from KOMODO-X on Wi-Fi. Test your body before a shoot

Connecting

  1. Put the RED on the network

    Either join the RED's Wi-Fi (or put the RED on the same network as the phone), or plug the USB-C tether in. The app prefers the wired path when both exist.

  2. Search

    On the Connection page tap RED · USB-C / Wi-Fi (Pro mode) or let Simple mode find it. The app probes the local subnet for RED's control port; no address is typed.

  3. Connect

    The row takes the camera's name; tap it. Connecting a RED disconnects any Nikon, and the reverse, outside Multicam.

The Connection page then shows the RED section with Open RED Controls and Disconnect. There is no manual IP entry for RED, and only one RED session at a time.

Controls

The Controls page shows RED tiles whose option lists come from the camera's own firmware, so they match what the camera menu offers. A tile is read only when the camera reports no list, and shows "Waiting for RED camera confirmation" while a change is pending.

The record card shows READY or RECORDING, the camera's timecode and MEDIA n% REMAINING. The status strip shows the battery percentage. The Focus section and the Audio / TC group are not shown for RED.

Live View

  • The top chips (battery, aperture, shutter, ISO, Kelvin, tint, format, codec) open the same RED pickers.
  • Every monitoring tool works on the RED picture: LUT, scopes, false color, zebras, X-Lights, peaking, guides, desqueeze. The exposure tools calibrate to Log3G10 and follow the ISO.
  • Autofocus: an AF capsule shows Single, Continuous or Off; tap it to change. The camera's own focus and face boxes are drawn; with Touch AF on, tap or drag to move the user box. Single mode runs one autofocus; Continuous keeps tracking. The box size is set by the camera, because the protocol has no command to change it.
  • Audio meters come from the camera's VU data.
  • Not shown for RED: TIME, PULL, MF, LENS, the REMAIN chip, and the Nikon Mode and EV chips.

LUTs and Log View

RED cameras can apply their own look to the live stream. To avoid applying two looks, turning on an app LUT saves the camera's state, enables Log View and turns the camera's LUT off, so the app's LUT and tools see the log picture. Turning the app LUT off, or disconnecting, restores the camera's settings. The bundled IPP2 Rec709 and RED Creative folders are built for RED's Log3G10. See LUTs and looks.

Multicam

A connected RED appears as a tile beside the Nikon bodies, joins group record, takes ISO, shutter, aperture, Kelvin and tint from the shared rail, and receives the timecode jam as a manual internal value. The RED's REC RUN or FREE RUN mode cannot be set over the protocol; set it on the camera. See Multicam.

Playback remote

With a RED connected, the Playback page lists the camera's clips (name, duration, format, compression, start timecode) and plays them through the camera's own decoder, streaming the result to the phone. Controls: previous clip, back 24 frames, play/pause, forward 24 frames, next clip, refresh. Nothing is downloaded and R3D files cannot be transferred, deleted or exported from the phone. If the firmware does not expose playback control the page says so.

Not supported for RED

  • Still capture, subject detection, audio input settings, card slot selection, the camera level.
  • Focus Pull and Manual Focus Drive; resizing the AF box.
  • Downloading, deleting, rating or exporting R3D clips; thumbnails.
  • Casting the RED picture to Remote Viewers; HDMI capture as the picture source.
  • Live Activities.
  • More than one RED at a time, and older DSMC or DSMC2 bodies.
