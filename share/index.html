Exposure Confidence

Traffic lights, goal posts, and scopes tuned for your signal.

Channel-aware traffic lights give you a fast read on highlight and shadow pressure before a take goes sideways. Pair them with waveform and histogram scopes tuned for confident exposure decisions.

Signal-aware calibration for Log3G10, N-Log, and SDR monitoring workflows - built around how the Nikon ZR is actually shot.

  • Traffic lights & goal posts
  • False color with legend
  • Waveform with exposure guidelines
  • Zebras: highlights, shadows, mid-gray, all

Focus Control

Manual focus, pull moves, and cinematic AF from live view.

Use ZR Ctl as a dedicated focus surface: tap to set focus, nudge manual focus in fine or coarse steps, and keep focus controls available without touching the camera body.

The pull tool helps you rehearse repeatable moves and execute them cleanly, while cinematic AF controls make focus transitions feel more intentional from the phone or tablet.

  • Fine and coarse manual focus controls
  • Pull tool for repeatable focus moves
  • Cinematic AF trigger from live view
  • AF area, subject area, and focus-box feedback

Focus Peaking

Peaking that stays available in demanding ZR recording modes.

For manual lenses, macro work, and R3D NE or RAW setups, ZR Ctl keeps edge peaking visible directly over live view so you can see what is actually biting.

Dial sensitivity and color to match the scene, then pair peaking with scopes, LUT preview, and framing tools without losing your focus reference.

  • Peaking for Nikon ZR R3D NE workflows
  • Adjustable sensitivity and color
  • Fine calibration for busy or low-contrast scenes
  • Works alongside scopes, LUT preview, and frame guides

Framing & Desqueeze

Anamorphic desqueeze with custom ratios. Frame guides for every delivery.

Desqueeze 1.33x, 1.5x, 1.8x, 2x, or enter a custom ratio — right in live view. Stack multiple frame guides with dashed comparison lines for multi-delivery shoots.

Built-in cinema, vertical, and social ratios. Golden ratio, thirds, center cross, safe areas. Full control over matte and guide opacity.

  • Custom anamorphic desqueeze input
  • Multiple simultaneous frame guides
  • 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 2.39, custom ratios
  • Matte & guide opacity controls

Playback

Review takes over WiFi without leaving the app.

Scan the camera card, see thumbnails, stream playable MP4/MOV clips, jump between takes, scrub, check audio, and preview with your monitoring LUT — all over WiFi, without touching the camera.

No more crowding around the body to check a take. Review instantly, keep moving.

  • Camera-card scan with thumbnails
  • Full-screen playback & scrubber
  • LUT preview during playback
  • Volume, clip navigation, audio check

And That's Not All

Every control, every tool.

23+ camera controls, a fully customizable interface, full audio monitoring, and LUT workflows designed for how the ZR is actually being shot.

Fully Customizable Interface

Presets, customizable status chips, tool pills, modern/monitor/classic HUD styles. Landscape toolbar placement, rotation lock, and home-indicator-safe controls.

Full Audio Controls

Real-time audio level meters, input sensitivity and gain adjustment, all controllable from live view. Monitor audio without a separate mixer.

LUTs Without Losing Exposure

Preview your LUT while exposure tools read the pre-LUT signal. A monitoring look should never hide clipped channels. Bundled Rec.709 conversion profiles plus custom .cube import.

Deep Cinema Controls

Shutter angle, dual base ISO, extended ISO, codec, resolution, frame rate, Kelvin, timecode, card slot, record start/stop — controls Nikon hasn't exposed anywhere else.

Near Zero Latency USB-C Monitoring

Direct USB connection for near-latency-free monitoring with all tools active. Waveform, false color, peaking, and LUTs over a simple cable.

Built for the Field

Bonjour discovery, direct AP connection, quick reconnect, transport switching, and support log capture. Designed for the messy parts of real camera days.
