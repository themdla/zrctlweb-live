FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where did ZR Ctl go?

ZR Ctl is now Parallax Monitor on every platform: iPhone, iPad and Android. It is the same app from the same developer. Your purchase carries over and there is nothing to buy again. After the update, the Android app appears under "Parallax Monitor" with a new icon.

How do I control a Nikon ZR remotely?

Install Parallax Monitor on your iPhone, iPad or Android device and connect to the camera over USB, your phone's hotspot, a shared WiFi network, or the camera's own access point. You get live view with scopes, full camera settings, focus control, audio, and camera-card playback — no extra hardware required.

Does Parallax Monitor work with the Nikon Z8 and Z6III?

Yes. Parallax Monitor is built for the Nikon ZR first, and Z8 and Z6III shooters have confirmed it working as a remote monitor and control surface on their bodies. Other Z-series cameras may work too — we'd love to hear your results.

Where can I get Parallax Monitor?

iPhone and iPad: Parallax Monitor is available now on the App Store. Android: Parallax Monitor for Android is available now on Google Play for Android phones and tablets.

Do you offer refunds for paid app purchases?

It depends on the store. On Google Play we offer a 30-day, no-questions-asked full refund: in the first 48 hours, request it in Google Play, and after that email us your order number and we refund it ourselves. On the App Store, Apple handles and decides refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can’t issue App Store refunds ourselves, but email us and we will help.

How do I apply to beta test Parallax Monitor?

Use the beta tester application to share your iPhone, iPad or Android device, Nikon cameras, shooting background, and email address. Beta access is reviewed from those applications.

Can I shoot stills with Parallax Monitor, or is it video-only?

Parallax Monitor has a full photo mode: dedicated stills control pages for exposure, capture, storage, and focus, a remote shutter from live view, and instant photo review over WiFi.

What monitoring tools are included?

Waveform, histogram, false color with legend, channel-aware traffic lights and goal posts, zebras, focus peaking (including R3D NE workflows), anamorphic desqueeze, custom frame guides, LUT preview with .cube import, and real-time audio meters — with ultra low-latency monitoring over USB.
