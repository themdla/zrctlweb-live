  • False color with legend

02

Focus control

Pull focus from
the palm of your hand.

Tap to set focus, nudge manual focus in fine or coarse steps, rehearse repeatable pull moves, and trigger cinematic AF — without touching the camera body.

  • Fine & coarse manual focus
  • Pull tool for repeatable moves
  • Cinematic AF trigger
  • Focus peaking in R3D NE & RAW

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I control a Nikon ZR remotely?

Install Parallax Monitor on your iPhone or iPad and connect to the camera over USB, your phone's hotspot, a shared WiFi network, or the camera's own access point. You get live view with scopes, full camera settings, focus control, audio, and camera-card playback — no extra hardware required.

Does Parallax Monitor work with the Nikon Z8 and Z6III?

Yes. Parallax Monitor is built for the Nikon ZR first, and Z8 and Z6III shooters have confirmed it working as a remote monitor and control surface on their bodies. Other Z-series cameras may work too — we'd love to hear your results.

Where can I get Parallax Monitor?

Parallax Monitor is available now on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

How do I apply to beta test Parallax Monitor?

Use the beta tester application to share your iPhone or iPad, Nikon cameras, shooting background, and email address. Beta access is reviewed from those applications.

Does Parallax Monitor have a refund policy?

Yes. Paid Parallax Monitor purchases are covered by a 30-day, no-questions-asked full refund promise. App Store refund requests begin through Apple, and support is available if you need help.

Is ZR Ctl for iOS now Parallax Monitor?

Yes. If you are looking for ZR Ctl on iPhone or iPad, the iOS app is now called Parallax Monitor.

Can I shoot stills with Parallax Monitor, or is it video-only?

Parallax Monitor has a full photo mode: dedicated stills control pages for exposure, capture, storage, and focus, a remote shutter from live view, and instant photo review over WiFi.

What monitoring tools are included?

Waveform, histogram, false color with legend, channel-aware traffic lights and goal posts, zebras, focus peaking (including R3D NE workflows), anamorphic desqueeze, custom frame guides, LUT preview with .cube import, and real-time audio meters — with ultra low-latency monitoring over USB.
