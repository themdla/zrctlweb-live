Before you submit

The details that make reports actionable.

You do not need every item, but complete reports are much easier to reproduce and resolve.

  • Your setup: app platform and version, device, OS, camera model, and camera firmware.
  • Connection: USB or Wi-Fi, plus any adapter, hub, or network details that matter.
  • What happened: concise steps, what you expected, and what actually happened.
  • Evidence: screenshots, screen recordings, or a support log when available.

Please do not submit passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other sensitive information. Feedback is processed through Tally as described in the Privacy Policy.
