Tap to set focus, nudge manual focus in fine or coarse steps, rehearse repeatable pull moves, and trigger cinematic AF — without touching the camera body.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I control a Nikon ZR remotely?

Install ZR Ctl on your Android device and connect to the camera over USB, your phone's hotspot, a shared WiFi network, or the camera's own access point. You get live view with scopes, full camera settings, focus control, audio, and camera-card playback — no extra hardware required.

Does ZR Ctl work with the Nikon Z8 and Z6III?

Yes. ZR Ctl is built for the Nikon ZR first, and Z8 and Z6III shooters have confirmed it working as a remote monitor and control surface on their bodies. Other Z-series cameras may work too — we'd love to hear your results.

How do I apply to beta test ZR Ctl for Android?

Use the beta tester application to share your Android device, Nikon cameras, shooting background, and email address. Beta access is reviewed from those applications.

Does ZR Ctl for Android have a refund policy?

Paid ZR Ctl Android purchases are covered by a 30-day, no-questions-asked full refund promise when paid access is available. Support is available if you need help.

Can I shoot stills with ZR Ctl, or is it video-only?

ZR Ctl has a full photo mode: dedicated stills control pages for exposure, capture, storage, and focus, a remote shutter from live view, and instant photo review over WiFi.

What monitoring tools are included?

Waveform, histogram, false color with legend, channel-aware traffic lights and goal posts, zebras, focus peaking (including R3D NE workflows), anamorphic desqueeze, custom frame guides, LUT preview with .cube import, and real-time audio meters — with ultra low-latency monitoring over USB.
